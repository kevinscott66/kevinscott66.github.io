# Audience and conversion checks

The homepage primarily serves clients with workflow or product problems. Developer collaboration and employment have separate entry points. Engineering details remain in case studies. This positioning is a hypothesis, not a measured conversion improvement.

## Quick research session

Show the homepage to prospective clients, developers and hiring managers separately. Do not explain the site first. Ask:

1. What does this person offer, and who is it for?
2. Which project is most relevant to a problem you have?
3. What evidence makes the work credible? What remains unclear?
4. How would you start a project or propose collaboration?
5. What would stop you from getting in touch?

Record the participant’s audience, paraphrase of the offer, route used, unanswered questions and blockers. Collect actual observations before changing priorities again. Do not treat teammates or AI reviews as customer research.
